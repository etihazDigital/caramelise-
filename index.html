Our Story

Sweetness, made to order

Caramélisé is a home-grown chocolaterie & pâtisserie cloud kitchen — every cake, chocolate and dessert is prepared fresh once you order it, not pulled off a shelf. That means your celebration gets a dessert made specifically around your flavour, size and design brief.

From delicate pâtisserie to statement celebration cakes and gifting-worthy chocolate hampers, the studio works closely with each customer over WhatsApp and Instagram to get every detail — from the flavour to the finishing touches — just right.
